## 🇺🇸 English

### Who I am

I'm Gustavo, a Full Stack developer and intern building my tech career from **Leopoldina, Minas Gerais, Brazil** ✝️.

Making a feature work is not enough for me. I want to understand **why** a solution exists, what it costs in architecture, and how it behaves under real use. That's what separates code that works today from software you can maintain tomorrow.

### My purpose

To build **reliable** software that solves real problems and opens doors: for the people who use it and for the region I come from.

### How I work

- **Understand the why before the how.** A technical decision without a reason becomes debt.
